# NuxGame — removed at their request

This repository previously held an API Evangelist profile of NuxGame. **It was removed
on 21 August 2026 at the request of NuxGame, and will not be restored.**

There is no profile, no `apis.yml`, no specification, no screenshot and no rating here.
This repository is kept only so that inbound links to this URL do not dangle.

## What happened

The Legal Department of NUXGEN B.V. (brand name NuxGame) wrote on 21 August 2026 asking
that this repository and the NuxGame-related materials in it be removed in their
entirety, and stating that NuxGame does not consent to the reproduction, publication or
maintenance of copies of its website materials.

The request was honored the same day, in full. Removed here and everywhere downstream:

- the provider profile and its single API entry
- the blogs, finops, kin, llms, plans, rate-limits and security artifact trees
- a screenshot of nuxgame.com, which is the material NuxGame specifically named
- the provider pages on apis.io and on providers.apievangelist.com
- the record in the apis.io search index
- the icon we hosted on S3

NuxGame is recorded in the catalog's delisting registry, which every pipeline consults
before it can create, enrich, score or index a provider. That entry — not the deletion
of these files — is what stops a future harvest or re-submission from recreating this
record automatically.

## On the request itself

NuxGame did not need to make the case they made, and we did not ask them to prove
standing. Removal is honored on request, at no charge, from anyone who asks. Their
letter quoted our own words back at us — "Want the listing taken down? Say so, and we
will honor it" — and that promise is real.

## Removal requests generally

If you represent a company listed in this catalog and want to be removed, email
kin@apievangelist.com. Removal is honored on request and at no charge. You do not need
a lawyer, a trademark registration, or a reason.
